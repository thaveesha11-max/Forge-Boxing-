About the club

Two bags and a borrowed timer.

That’s how Forge started, in a two-car garage in 2019. Today it’s 6,000 square feet, six coaches and a full timetable. The standard hasn’t changed.

We wanted a gym where a nervous beginner and a fighter two weeks out could share the same floor and both get coached properly. Dante Cruz, founder

Why we built it

Dante spent ten years coaching in other people’s gyms. The good ones were built for fighters and quietly ignored everyone else. The busy ones sold “boxing fitness” that never taught anyone to box.

Forge was his answer: real technique for everyone, taught in the same order a fighter learns it, with coaches who know the difference between pushing someone and breaking them.

Eleven members signed up in the garage. Most of them still train here. The club moved to Foundry Road in 2020 and has grown one class, one coach and one bag at a time since.

How we got here

  1. 2019

    Forge opens in a two-car garage with two heavy bags and 11 members.

  2. 2020

    We move to 88 Foundry Road and build our first proper ring.

  3. 2022

    Maya launches Women’s Boxing. It fills in its first month.

  4. 2023

    The Fight Team forms and our first boxers compete.

  5. 2024

    Jonah starts Youth Boxing for ages 8 to 15.

  6. 2026

    We double the floor to 6,000 square feet and 14 heavy bags.

House rules

Written on the wall by the ring.

Six rules keep the floor safe, friendly and serious about boxing. Coaches enforce every one.

  1. Leave your ego at the door

    Everyone here was a beginner once. Help the new person. Never test them.

  2. Touch gloves

    Before every round and after it. Respect your partner every single time.

  3. The coach sets the pace

    In sparring, the coach decides the intensity. No exceptions.

  4. Ask questions

    Coaches would rather explain something twice than watch you guess.

  5. Rack your gear

    Wipe the bag, rack the weights and keep gloves off the floor.

  6. Show up

    Consistency beats talent. Two classes a week will change you.
