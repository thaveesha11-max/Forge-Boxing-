Every coach here has boxed, and every one of them teaches beginners. You’ll train with most of them in your first month.

Founder · Head coach

Dante Cruz

“Anyone can throw punches. Boxing is knowing when not to.”

Dante boxed 64 amateur bouts over nine years, then spent a decade coaching in other people’s gyms. He opened Forge in a two-car garage in 2019 with two bags and a borrowed ring timer. He still teaches the first class of every Technical Boxing block himself.
