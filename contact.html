What to bring

We lend gloves and wraps, and a coach wraps your hands for you. You just need four things.

Water

A full bottle. There’s a filtered refill station by the bags.

Flat trainers

Court or cross-training shoes. Thick running soles make footwork harder.

Clothes to sweat in

Shorts or leggings and a T-shirt. Nothing with zips or buckles.

A towel

You’ll need it. Showers and hair dryers are in the locker room.
