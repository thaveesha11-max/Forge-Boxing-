The Forge method

Steel takes three stages. So do fighters.

A blacksmith heats, hammers, then tempers. Every program here follows the same order, so you always know what you’re working on and why.

Your free class

Your first hour, minute by minute.

Nobody gets thrown in the deep end. This is exactly what happens when you book a free Foundations class.

  1. −15:00

    Arrive

    Check in at the desk. A coach wraps your hands and fits you with gloves.

  2. 00:00

    Warm up

    Skipping, shadowboxing and mobility to get you loose.

  3. 00:10

    Stance & jab

    Feet, guard and your first punch. The coach checks every rep.

  4. 00:30

    Bag rounds

    Two-minute rounds putting the jab and cross together, a minute’s rest between.

  5. 00:50

    Core & stretch

    Ten minutes of core work, then a proper cool-down.

  6. 01:00

    Talk it over

    Tell us how it felt and what you want from boxing. No hard sell.
