Questions

Before you sign up.

Something we haven’t covered? Call (555) 010-4477 or ask at the desk.

Do I need any boxing experience?

No. Most of our members had never thrown a punch before their first class. Foundations is built for complete beginners, and a coach will look after you from the moment you arrive.

Will I have to spar?

Only if you want to. Sparring happens in one class, Sparring Lab, and you need a coach’s approval to join it. Every other class is pads, bags and drills with no contact.

What should I bring to my first class?

Water, trainers with flat soles and clothes you can sweat in. We lend gloves and wraps for free until you buy your own.

How fit do I need to be?

You don’t need to be fit to start. Coming is how you get fit. Every drill has an easier and a harder version, and coaches will scale anything you need.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?

You can freeze for up to two months a year. Monthly plans cancel with 30 days’ notice and no fees.

Yearly plans are paid up front. You can freeze them, and you can cancel for a full refund within the first 14 days.

Is there a minimum age?

Youth Boxing is for ages 8 to 15. Adult classes are for 16 and over, and 16 and 17 year olds need a parent to sign the waiver.

Do you offer discounts?

Students, teachers and first responders get 10% off any membership. Siblings save $15 a month on Youth. Ask at the desk with ID.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. Anyone’s first class is free. Unlimited members also get a monthly guest pass, and Fight Team members get two.

Try before you pick.

Your first class is free. Train once, then choose a plan with a coach who has seen you box.
