Questions

Before you join.

Anything else? Call +94 77 000 0000 or send us a message.

Is there a contract?

No. Memberships run month to month. Renew whenever you want, and stop whenever you need to.

What counts as a day?

One visit to any session on the timetable. Starter gives you 8 of those a month, Regular gives you 12, and Unlimited has no cap.

Do I need boxing experience?

No. Forge welcomes anyone with a genuine interest in the sport. The coach will build you up from the basics.

What should I bring?

Water, a towel, trainers and clothes you can sweat in. Ask us about gloves and hand wraps before your first session.

How do kids and private sessions work?

Both are arranged directly with us. Get in touch and we’ll share times and pricing.
