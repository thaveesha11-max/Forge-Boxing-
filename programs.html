Eight ways in.

From your first jab to your first bout. Every class is coached, and every class has a heat rating so you know what you’re walking into.

Book a free class →

How to read the heat rating

A smith judges steel by its colour. We use the same chart to rate how hard a class runs.

  1. 1,200°FDull red

    Self-paced. Technique and light work.

  2. 1,500°FCherry

    Steady. Built for beginners and kids.

  3. 1,750°FOrange

    Working hard, with room to think.

  4. 2,000°FYellow

    Hard rounds. Expect to breathe heavily.

  5. 2,300°FWhite

    Full intensity, including live sparring.

BeginnerStage 01 · Heat

Foundations

Stance, footwork, the jab-cross and basic defense. Every member starts here, including people who have boxed before.

Length
60 minutes
Heat
Cherry · 1,500°F
Class size
Up to 16, two coaches
Runs
13 times a week
Contact
None. Pads and bags only.
Try Foundations free →

Foundations teaches boxing in the order it’s built: feet first, then guard, then punches, then defense. Each week has a theme, so after a month you have the full set of basics and the habits to use them.

Two coaches run every class. One leads, one walks the floor fixing stances and wrists. If you’ve never trained before, this is the only class you need for your first month.

Inside a class · minutes to scale

102012126
  • Warm-up 10
  • Technique 20
  • Partner drills 12
  • Bag rounds 12
  • Core & cool-down 6

After a month you can

  • Hold a proper stance and guard while moving
  • Throw a clean jab, cross, hook and uppercut
  • Block, parry and slip the basic attacks
  • Get through six rounds on the bag

Good fit if

  • You’ve never boxed, or it’s been years
  • You want technique before intensity
  • You’re 16 or over (see Youth for younger)

Fight Conditioning

Bag rounds, bodyweight circuits and fight-pace drills. No contact and no experience needed.

Length
45 minutes
Heat
Yellow · 2,000°F
Class size
Up to 20
Runs
12 times a week, from 6 AM
Contact
None
Try Conditioning free →

Boxers are some of the fittest athletes alive because they train in rounds: work hard for three minutes, recover for one, repeat. Fight Conditioning uses the same structure with the bag, the floor and the clock.

Rafa builds every session and posts it on the board, so you’ll see exactly how many rounds are left. You’ll work at your own pace, and the coach will scale anything you need.

Inside a class · minutes to scale

691812
  • Warm-up 6
  • Fight-pace drills 9
  • Bag rounds 18
  • Circuits & core 12

What you’ll feel

  • Better recovery between hard efforts
  • Stronger shoulders, legs and core
  • Faster, sharper hands on the bag

Good fit if

  • You want the workout without the contact
  • You like a clock and a clear finish line
  • You box already and need more engine

IntermediateStage 03 · Temper

Technical Boxing

Combinations, counters and ring craft. Most of the class is on the pads with a coach.

Length
75 minutes
Heat
Orange · 1,750°F
Class size
Up to 14
Runs
Mon, Wed and Fri evenings
Entry
About three months of Foundations, or a coach’s sign-off
Ask about joining →

Once the basics are automatic, Technical Boxing teaches you to put them together under pressure. You’ll learn to set traps, cut angles, counter off defense and read what your opponent is about to do.

Dante runs this class the way he runs fight camp: one idea per session, drilled until it shows up in your rounds without you thinking about it.

Inside a class · minutes to scale

102025128
  • Warm-up 10
  • Technique 20
  • Pad work 25
  • Bag & shadow rounds 12
  • Conditioning 8

You’ll work on

  • Five and six punch combinations
  • Counters off slips, rolls and parries
  • Footwork to cut off the ring
  • Feints and changing rhythm

Good fit if

  • Foundations feels comfortable now
  • You want to spar one day, or already do
  • You like detail and repetition

Beginners train alongside members who have competed, and the coaches split drills by level so everyone is challenged. Plenty of members start here and later add Foundations or Sparring Lab.

Kids learn the same fundamentals as adults, broken into games, challenges and short rounds. They earn coloured wraps as they pass each skill check, which does wonders for focus.

Jonah leads every session with a second coach on the floor. Parents are welcome to watch from the bench, and we send a short progress note home each month.

Coach approvalStage 03 · Temper

Sparring Lab

Controlled, coached sparring with partners matched on size and experience. The safest way to learn to box for real.

Length
75 minutes
Heat
White · 2,300°F
Class size
Up to 12, six pairs
Runs
Tue and Thu, 7:15 PM
Gear
16 oz gloves, headguard, mouthguard
Ask about sparring →

Nobody spars at Forge until a coach says they’re ready. Before your first round you’ll do situational sparring, where each partner has one job, like jab only or defend only, so you get used to being in range without the chaos.

Tessa sets the intensity for every round and stops anything that gets out of hand. The goal is learning, not winning, and partners who forget that don’t get paired again.

Inside a class · minutes to scale

121015308
  • Warm-up 12
  • Technique 10
  • Situational sparring 15
  • Sparring rounds 30
  • Cool-down 8

The path in

  • Three months of regular training
  • A skills check with any coach
  • Situational sparring before open rounds

House rules

  • Coach sets the intensity, every round
  • Touch gloves before and after
  • Headguards on for all members

Our competitive squad. Fight camps, bout matching and a coach in your corner on the night.

Length
90 minutes

Fight Team boxers compete in amateur and white-collar bouts. The squad trains together on Fridays and joins Technical Boxing and Sparring Lab during the week.

Before a bout you get an eight-week camp with a plan for sparring, conditioning and weight. We handle registration, medicals and matchmaking, and a Forge coach works your corner.

Length

Book a private session →

An hour with a coach and nobody else changes how fast you improve. We’ll film a few rounds, pick the two or three things holding you back and fix them on the pads.

Privates are also the easiest way to start if a group class feels like too much. Many members book a block of five before their first class, then keep one a month to stay sharp.

Popular for

  • Fixing one specific habit
  • Bout preparation and game plans
  • Couples or friends training together

How booking works

  • We match you with the right coach
  • Sessions run any time we’re open
