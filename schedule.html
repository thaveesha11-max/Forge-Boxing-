First visit

Which class should I book?

Book Foundations, Fight Conditioning, Women’s Boxing or Youth Boxing. All four are built for people who have never trained, and a coach will look after you from the moment you walk in.

  • Arrive 15 minutes early for your first class so we can wrap your hands.
  • Wear trainers with flat soles and clothes you can move in.
  • We lend gloves and wraps for free until you get your own.
